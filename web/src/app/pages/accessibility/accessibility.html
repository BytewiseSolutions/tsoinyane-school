Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to an inclusive digital experience

Our Commitment

Tsoinyane Government Combined School is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all users, including those with disabilities.

Accessibility Features

We strive to meet accessibility standards by using clear and readable fonts, sufficient colour contrast, descriptive image alt text, and keyboard-navigable content.

Known Limitations

While we aim for full accessibility, some areas of the website may still be in the process of being improved. We are continuously working to enhance the experience for all users.

Feedback

If you experience any accessibility barriers on our website, please contact us at info@tsoinyane.co.ls or call +266 59181664 so we can address the issue promptly.
