Who We Are

Tsoinyane Government Combined School is a public institution located in Tsoinyane, Pitseng, Leribe. We are committed to providing quality education to the children of our community through dedicated teaching and a supportive learning environment.

We are a combined school with both Primary and High School operating in the same yard, serving hundreds of students from the surrounding villages.

Our Mission

To provide inclusive, quality education that empowers every student to reach their full potential and contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Vision

To be a centre of academic excellence and character development in the Leribe district and beyond.

Our Values

Integrity, respect, excellence, community, and lifelong learning are the values that guide everything we do.
